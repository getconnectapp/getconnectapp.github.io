Is Connect free?

Yes, completely. There is no paid plan and nothing to buy.

Is it on Android?

Not yet. Connect is on iPhone today, and Android is coming to Google Play.

Who made Connect?

Mahdi Saiah, a product designer and product owner in Paris. He designed and launched it on his own, from the first user interviews to the App Store. See his portfolio.

Who can join?

Adults only, 18 and over. The whole app works in English and French.

Is my data sold?

No. Connect does not sell your personal data. The privacy policy explains the rest in plain words.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings, Delete account, and confirm. Your profile, posts and messages go with it.

I have a problem with the app.

Go to Settings, Report a problem in the app, or write to connectappfr@gmail.com. Replies usually come within 2 business days.
